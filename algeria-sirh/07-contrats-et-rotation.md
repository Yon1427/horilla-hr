# 07 — Contrats de travail & rotation des équipes (Algérie, secteur privé)

**Scope:** private sector only (loi 90-11). Public-sector statutes and concours are
out of scope. This file deepens `02 §1` (contracts) and `03 §3` (time and
attendance); it does not replace them.

**Legend:** `OFF` official text (JO/CNAS/MTESS), `REP` reputable secondary,
`PRAC` practice, `UNV` unverified / sources conflict.

---

# PART A — Types de contrat de travail

## A1. Legal framework

| Text | What it governs |
|---|---|
| **Loi n° 90-11 du 21 avril 1990** (JO n°17/1990), modified by loi 91-29, décret législatif 94-03, ord. 96-21, ord. 97-02 and 97-03 | Contract formation, CDI/CDD/part-time, trial, termination |
| **Ordonnance n° 97-03 du 11 janvier 1997** | Legal working time: 40 h/week, minimum 5 working days |
| **Décret exécutif n° 97-473 du 8 décembre 1997** | Part-time work (conditions, form, rights) |
| **Décret exécutif n° 97-474 du 8 décembre 1997** | Home workers (*travailleurs à domicile*) |
| **Loi n° 81-07 du 27 juin 1981** | Apprenticeship contract |
| **Loi n° 04-19 du 25 décembre 2004** | Placement of workers, ANEM, private placement agencies |
| **Loi n° 81-10 du 11 juillet 1981** + décret 82-510 | Foreign workers and work permits |
| **Loi n° 22-16 du 20 juillet 2022** + décret 22-352 | Unpaid leave / part-time to create a business (once per career) |

Fundamental rule (art. 8–11): the contract may be written or oral; proof is free.
A contract is **presumed indefinite (CDI)** unless the parties have provided
otherwise **in writing**. No written contract → CDI presumption. `[OFF]`

## A2. Contract types an Algerian private employer actually uses

| # | Type | Legal basis | Written form | Duration | Trial | End | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CDI** (contrat à durée indéterminée) | art. 11 | Not required; recommended | Unlimited | 6 months max; 12 for high qualification; set by CCN | art. 66 modes (resignation, dismissal, retirement, death, etc.) | The default. Written CDI is evidence, not a condition. |
| 2 | **CDD** (contrat à durée déterminée) | art. 12, 12 bis, 14 | **Required** | Fixed, stated in the contract | Same rules; must fit inside the CDD | End of term (art. 66) | Only in the 5 legal cases (A3). No statutory max or renewal cap. Breach → CDI. |
| 3 | **Temps partiel** | art. 13 + décret 97-473 | **Required** | CDI by default; CDD possible | Cannot exceed the full-time trial | Same as CDI/CDD | Volume < legal duration but **≥ half** (≥20 h/week). |
| 4 | **Apprentissage** | loi 81-07 | **Required**, signed by employer + apprentice + guardian, registered at the APC | 1–3 years | Specific apprenticeship regime | Expiry, death, incapacity, etc. (loi 81-07 arts. 23–24) | Age 15–18 (20 for disability); State-paid presalaire then employer; social contributions borne by the State. |
| 5 | **Travail à domicile** | décret 97-474 | Required regime | Usually CDI | Per contract | Per contract | Specific regime: place of work, equipment, remuneration, safety. |
| 6 | **Intérim / placement** | loi 04-19 | Contract with the user employer + agency contract | Per assignment | Per contract | End of assignment | There is **no general statutory "travail temporaire" contract type** in loi 90-11. Model the worker as a CDD/CDI of the agency or user; do not invent a statutory temp status. `[UNV]` |
| 7 | **Contrats aidés / insertion** (DAIP, CNAC, ANEM schemes) | scheme texts (e.g. décret 09-305, 22-85) | Required | Scheme-dependent | Scheme-dependent | Scheme-dependent | State-subsidised. A SIRH needs a distinct type, subsidy tracking and specific declarations; current 2026 rules must be checked scheme by scheme. `[UNV]` |
| — | **Stage** (internship) | — | Convention de stage | Fixed | — | End of studies | **Not an employment contract.** No CNAS affiliation as an employee unless the stage is a *stage d'insertion* / *contrat de professionnalisation* under a scheme. Do not pay it as salary. |
| — | **Étranger** | loi 81-10 | Work permit + contract | Per permit | Per contract | Per permit/contract | The employer must first check that no national labour is available (art. 21). Permit expiry is a blocking alert. |

> **CDI trial clarification:** art. 18 says **6 months max**, extendable to **12**
> for high-qualification posts, and fixed by collective bargaining per category.
> The MTESS consolidated text prints “dix (06) mois”; the rectificatif (JO n°38/1991)
> corrects it to **six (06)**. `[OFF]` Vendor pages saying “6 months for cadres,
> 3 months for others” are quoting a CCN, not the law. `[UNV]`

## A3. CDD: the five legal cases (art. 12 + ord. 96-21)

1. Work linked to **non-renewable works or service contracts**.
2. **Replacing** an employee who is temporarily absent and whose post must be kept.
3. **Periodic work of a discontinuous nature** for the employer.
4. **Temporary surge of work** or **seasonal** reasons.
5. **Activities or jobs of limited duration, or temporary by nature** (added by ord. 96-21).

Rules:
- The contract must state **the duration and the motive**. `[OFF]`
- **No statutory maximum duration and no statutory renewal cap.** Do not hard-code
  “2 years” or “one renewal”; a sector CCN may add one. `[OFF]` + `[UNV]` for CCNs.
- A CDD concluded outside these cases is **requalified as a CDI** (art. 14). `[OFF]`
- The labour inspector verifies the motive and the duration (art. 12 bis). `[OFF]`
- Case law: an abusive early termination of a CDD gives the employee the **remaining
  term + damages**; the “6 months” rule of art. 73-4 (abusive dismissal of a CDI)
  does not apply to a CDD. `[REP]` (Supreme Court, social chamber, 2018 decisions)
- “Prime de précarité” at the end of a CDD: **not in loi 90-11**; only a CCN or the
  contract can provide it. `[UNV]`

## A4. Part-time contract (art. 13 + décret 97-473)

- Definition: duration **below the legal duration** but **never below half** of it. `[OFF]`
- Two routes: employer needs it (lower workload) or employee asks for family/personal
  reasons and the employer accepts. `[OFF]`
- Written contract is required; without it the relationship is presumed **CDI**. `[OFF]`
- The employer must answer a written employee request **within 30 days** (arts. 4–6). `[REP]`
- **Equal rights** with full-time workers, **pro rata temporis**: leave, seniority,
  promotion, training, social protection. Seniority is counted **as if full-time**
  (art. 12). `[OFF]`
- Trial period cannot exceed the full-time trial (art. 13). `[OFF]`
- Eligible to the *comité de participation* (art. 14). `[OFF]`
- A CDD can also be part-time. `[OFF]`
- Special route: **part-time or unpaid leave to create a business**, once per career
  (loi 22-16 + décret 22-352). `[OFF]`

> SIRH consequence: part-time is **not** a contract *duration* type but a *volume*
> attribute. Model it as `contract_duration_type = CDI|CDD` **plus**
> `work_time_regime = FULL|PARTIAL` and `weekly_hours`. Leave and pay are prorated;
> seniority is not.

## A5. Trial period and confirmation

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum | 6 months; 12 months for high-qualification posts | art. 18 `[OFF]` |
| Per-category duration | Fixed by **collective bargaining**, not by the law | art. 18 `[OFF]` |
| Rights during trial | Same rights and obligations as similar employees | art. 19 `[OFF]` |
| Termination during trial | Either party, **any time, without notice and without indemnity** | art. 20 `[OFF]` |
| Seniority | Trial counts if the employee is confirmed | art. 19 `[OFF]` |
| CDD + trial | The trial must fit inside the CDD duration | practice `[UNV]` |

SIRH: never auto-confirm silently at trial end. Create a decision task and alert
HR at J-15/J-3. A termination during trial still needs a written reason and audit.

## A6. Mandatory content and language

The law does not impose a general list of CDI clauses. What must be written:
- **CDD**: duration + motive (art. 12).
- **Part-time**: written contract + volume and distribution (décret 97-473).
- **Apprenticeship**: written, signed by the three parties, APC-registered.
- **Payslip**: every pay element named and itemised (art. 86).

Recommended content (practice, and required by most CCNs): parties, position and
classification, place of work, start date, type and duration, trial, remuneration
and components, working time, leave, collective agreement reference, termination.

Language: loi 90-11 is silent. Arabic is the official language, French is standard
in private HR. Store `contract_language` and issue a bilingual template when the
employee is Arabic-only; state which version prevails. `[UNV]` for a legal obligation.

## A7. End of contract by type

| Type | Normal end | Early end | Indemnity |
|---|---|---|---|
| CDI | Resignation, dismissal, retirement, death, total incapacity, legal cessation (art. 66) | Dismissal requires a real and serious motive + procedure (art. 73) | **No statutory scale**: art. 72 (1 month/year, 15 max) was **abrogated** by décret 94-09 art. 35; the amount comes from the CCN/contract `[OFF]` |
| CDD | Arrival of the term | Abusive early end → remaining term + damages `[REP]` | No statutory “prime de précarité” `[UNV]` |
| Part-time | Same as CDI/CDD | Same | Pro rata |
| Apprenticeship | Expiry of the term | Listed cases / judicial | Per loi 81-07 |

Exit documents: **certificat de travail** (art. 67), attestation de salaire, final
settlement, CNAS cessation declaration.

## A8. SIRH model — contracts

```
Contract
  id, employee_id, legal_entity_id, establishment_id
  contract_type        ENUM CDI | CDD | APPRENTICESHIP | HOME_WORK | INTERIM | AIDED | STAGE
  duration_type        ENUM INDEFINITE | FIXED
  work_time_regime     ENUM FULL | PARTIAL
  weekly_hours         decimal          # >= 20 if PARTIAL
  cdd_motive_code      ENUM (5 art.12 cases)   # required if FIXED
  start_date, end_date, trial_start, trial_end
  notice_rule_id       FK -> CCN rule
  collective_agreement_id, contract_language
  signed_at, apc_registration_ref (apprenticeship), work_permit_ref (foreign)
  status               ENUM DRAFT | ACTIVE | EXPIRING | ENDED | ARCHIVED
```

Validations (block save, not warn):
- `FIXED` without `cdd_motive_code` or `end_date` → reject (art. 12/14).
- `PARTIAL` with `weekly_hours < 20` → reject (art. 13).
- `APPRENTICESHIP` outside age 15–18 (20 with disability) → reject.
- `FOREIGN` without a valid permit → block activation.
- CDD chain for the same post → warning + legal approval (requalification risk).
- Trial longer than 6 months (12 high qualification) → reject unless the stored CCN
  rule proves a longer duration (the law's ceiling is 12). `[OFF]`

Alerts: CDD J-60/30/7; trial J-15/3; permit J-60/30; APC registration missing;
part-time request unanswered at D+30.

# PART B — Shift work and rotation

## B1. Legal basis

| Rule | Text | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Legal duration | Ord. 97-03 art. 2 | **40 h/week**, spread over **at least 5 working days** |
| Agriculture | loi 90-11 art. 24 | **2 000 h/year**, spread by periods |
| Daily amplitude | loi 90-11 art. 26 (restated in ord. 97-03 art. 7) | **12 h max** of effective work per day |
| Continuous shift pause | art. 25 + ord. 97-03 art. 6 | Break ≤ 1 h, of which **30 min counted as work** |
| Night work | art. 27 | **21:00–05:00**; conditions and rights set by CCN |
| Night work — minors | art. 28 | **Under 19: prohibited** |
| Night work — women | art. 29 | Prohibited unless the **labour inspector** grants a special derogation; literal text, flag for non-discrimination review |
| Shift work | art. 30 | Employer may organise **successive teams / travail posté** when production or service needs require it; **gives right to an indemnity** |
| Overtime | art. 31 | Absolute necessity, exceptional; **max 20% of legal duration = 8 h/week** |
| Overtime premium | art. 32 | **At least +50%** of the normal hourly wage |
| Weekly rest | art. 33 | **One full day**; normal day = **Friday** |
| Rest-day work | art. 36 | Work on a legal rest day → **compensatory rest of equal duration + overtime premium** |
| Rotating rest | art. 37 | Weekly rest may be **deferred/taken another day**; **rolling rest** is allowed where interrupting the activity is incompatible or prejudicial to the public |
| Premiums are salary | art. 81 | Shift, night, nuisance, on-call and zone indemnities are **salary** → CNAS base and IRG taxable |
| CCN content | art. 120 | CCNs set work norms, hours and their distribution, premiums |

## B2. Hard limits (enforce in the roster engine)

| Constraint | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly hours | 40 h (35 h is a CCN improvement, not the legal norm) | ord. 97-03 `[OFF]` |
| Minimum working days/week | 5 | ord. 97-03 `[OFF]` |
| Max effective work/day | **12 h** | art. 26 `[OFF]` |
| Minimum rest in a 24 h cycle | **12 h** (by implication of the 12 h amplitude; no separate article) | `[REP/PRAC]` |
| Overtime | **8 h/week (20%)**, exceptional | art. 31 `[OFF]` |
| Night window | 21:00–05:00 | art. 27 `[OFF]` |
| Weekly rest | 1 full day, Friday default; may rotate | arts. 33, 37 `[OFF]` |
| Under-19 at night | prohibited | art. 28 `[OFF]` |
| Ramadan (private) | no statutory reduction; company policy | `[UNV/PRAC]` |

> **The law does not fix:** a maximum night-shift length (the often-quoted “7 h”
> is secondary, not in loi 90-11), a minimum number of hours between two shifts
> beyond the 12 h amplitude, the rotation pattern (2-2-2, weekly, 3-3…), or the
> premium percentages. All four come from the **CCN, the règlement intérieur or
> the contract**. `[OFF absence]` + `[UNV]` for the specific figures.

## B3. Shift systems in practice

The standard typology (also used by Algerian CCNs):

| System | Coverage | Typical pattern | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2x8 discontinu** | Morning + afternoon, stop at weekend | 06:00–14:00 / 14:00–22:00 | Friday (+ Saturday in practice) |
| **3x8 semi-continuous** | 24 h, stop at weekend | 06–14 / 14–22 / 22–06 | Friday–Saturday |
| **3x8 continuous** | 24/7, including Friday and holidays | Same three shifts, rotating | **Rolling rest day** (art. 37) |
| **4x8 / 5x8** | 24/7 with more teams, shorter cycles | Sector-specific (oil & gas, utilities) | Rolling, often with long leave |
| **2x12** | 12 h shifts | 06–18 / 18–06 | Fewer working days; must respect the 12 h amplitude |

SIRH must treat the **pattern** as data (a cycle of shifts + rest days), not as code.

## B4. Shift premium (*indemnité de travail posté*, ITP)

- Art. 30 gives the **right**; the **rate** is not in the 1990 law. `[OFF]`
- The historical reference grid (décret of **3 February 1981**, JO n°5/1981) sets:

| System | ITP (% of base salary) |
|---|---|
| Continuous 3x8 (24/7) | **25%** |
| Semi-continuous 3x8 (24 h, stop at weekend) | **15%** |
| Discontinuous 2x8 (morning/afternoon, stop at weekend) | **10%** |
| “Périodes creuses” continuous / semi / discontinuous | **15% / 10% / 5%** |

  Source: JO n°5 of 3 Feb 1981 (décret fixing the ITP). `[OFF for the text]`
  **Applicability caveat:** this decree was adopted under the 1978 public-sector
  statute and is still cited; for a private employer in 2026 the binding rate is
  normally the **CCN**. Verify before using it as a default. `[UNV]`
- Real CCN example — Algérie Poste: art. 109 defines shift work as rotating teams;
  art. 110 lists the three systems (continuous 3x8, semi-continuous 3x8,
  discontinuous 2x8); art. 111 creates the **ITP** whose rate depends on the type
  of shift organisation. `[OFF example]`
- ITP **does not replace** overtime pay. `[REP]`
- ITP is **salary** under art. 81 → included in the CNAS base and taxable IRG. `[OFF]`
- ITP is not due for a one-off shift change; it compensates a **habitual shift
  organisation**. `[PRAC]`

## B5. Night work premium

- The law defines night (21:00–05:00) and delegates the **rights** to the CCN
  (art. 27). There is **no statutory percentage**. `[OFF]`
- Published figures conflict: one guide says 30%; another says 25%/50%/75%/100%
  depending on night/weekend/holiday; a third says a night shift is capped at 7 h.
  None of these is in loi 90-11. `[UNV — do not hard-code]`
- What the law does say: work on a **legal rest day** (Friday, public holiday) gives
  **compensatory rest of equal duration + the overtime premium** (art. 36). `[OFF]`
- Night work by **under-19** is prohibited (art. 28). `[OFF]`
- Night work by **women** is prohibited unless the labour inspector grants a
  derogation (art. 29). This is the literal text; implement it as a flag with the
  derogation reference, and get counsel's view on current non-discrimination
  enforcement. `[OFF / UNV]`

## B6. Weekly rest and rotation

- Normal weekly rest day = **Friday**; one full day (art. 33). `[OFF]`
- For continuous operations, the rest may be **deferred or taken another day**, and
  **rolling rest** is expressly allowed where stopping the activity is incompatible
  with its nature or prejudicial to the public (art. 37). `[OFF]`
- Working on the weekly rest day or a public holiday → **compensatory rest of equal
  duration + overtime premium** (art. 36). `[OFF]`
- Do not assume Saturday is a rest day for a private employer: it is practice/CCN,
  not the statutory default (see `04 §3.1`).

## B7. Rest between shifts

There is **no separate article** fixing a minimum daily rest. The 12 h daily
amplitude (art. 26) means a worker cannot work more than 12 h in a day, which in a
24 h cycle leaves at least 12 h. A CCN may require more (e.g. 11 h or 12 h
consecutive). Enforce **12 h between the end of one shift and the start of the
next** as the safe default. `[REP/PRAC]`

## B8. Ramadan

- **Private sector:** no general statutory reduction of working time. The employer
  sets the timetable by internal rules or CCN; a 1–2 h reduction is common but not a
  legal right. `[UNV/PRAC]`
- Pay is not reduced because of Ramadan hours. Hours worked beyond the Ramadan
  timetable are overtime under the normal rules.
- Model a `ramadan_timetable` per establishment, with start/end from the official
  announcement (see `04 §3.4`).

## B9. Worked example (illustrative)

A 3x8 **continuous** team, 40 h/week, base salary 60 000 DA, ITP 25%, one public
holiday worked:

| Line | Amount | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Base salary | 60 000,00 | — |
| ITP 25% | 15 000,00 | 1981 grid / CCN (verify) |
| Shift pay total | 75 000,00 | ITP is salary (art. 81) |
| Holiday work (7 h) | 7 h × hourly × 1.50 | art. 36: compensatory rest + premium |
| Compensatory rest | 7 h | Must be scheduled, not paid away |
| Overtime | 0 (within 40 h) | Cap 8 h/week if used |

CNAS and IRG are computed on the **75 000 + holiday premium**, not on the base
salary alone, because shift and night premiums are salary. `[OFF art. 81]`


Validators (block the roster, not just warn):
- planned effective hours > 12 in a day → reject (art. 26).
- < 12 h between two shifts → reject (default).
- weekly hours > 40 (excluding approved overtime) → warn/block by policy.
- overtime > 8 h/week → block or require exception reason + representative
  consultation + inspector notification (art. 31).
- a continuous pattern without a rolling rest day → reject (art. 37).
- under-19 assigned to NIGHT → reject (art. 28).
- female employee assigned to NIGHT without an inspector derogation reference →
  block and require the reference (art. 29; counsel review).
- rest-day or holiday work without a compensatory-rest entry → block (art. 36).
- ITP applied to overtime hours → warn (ITP is not overtime pay).

Reports: hours by shift, night hours, ITP cost, overtime cap breaches, rest-day
compensation owed, rotation fairness (nights per employee per month).
